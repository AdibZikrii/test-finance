    Frequently asked questions

    Is this a real loan application?

    No. Meridian Trust Bank is fictional and this site is a demonstration. No applications are processed.

    Is my information saved?

    No. Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent over the network.

    How is the decision made?

    The score combines credit score (40 points), debt-to-income ratio (30), employment (15), loan size versus annual income (10) and age (5). Payments are estimated at a fixed 8% APR.

    What is debt-to-income (DTI)?

    Your monthly debt payments, including the new loan, divided by your monthly gross income. Lower is better; this demo likes it under 36%.
